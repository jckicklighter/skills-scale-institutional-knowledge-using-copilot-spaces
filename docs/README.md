# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation. This folder is the central repository for the processes, roles, and working rhythms that guide how OctoAcme plans, delivers, and improves projects across product, engineering, and stakeholder teams.

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative project management approach that emphasizes clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and continuous learning. The goal is to help teams move from idea to delivery with shared expectations, strong communication, and a repeatable process for execution.

## Core Principles

- Customer-first: prioritize customer value, usability, and business impact
- Iterative delivery: deliver small, testable increments to learn early and reduce risk
- Clear ownership: name accountable leads for delivery, product direction, and decision-making
- Data-informed decisions: use metrics, feedback, and evidence to guide scope and prioritization
- Psychological safety: encourage honest communication, learning, and continuous improvement

## Project Lifecycle

Every OctoAcme project follows a consistent lifecycle:

1. Initiation — validate the business need, align stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed
2. Planning — define scope, milestones, dependencies, and backlog priorities
3. Execution — build, test, review, and iterate while tracking progress and blockers
4. Release — confirm readiness and deploy with checks, rollback plans, and stakeholder communication
5. Close & Retrospective — capture lessons learned and convert improvements into action

## Quick Reference

### Common Artifacts

- Project One-pager / charter
- Roadmap and release plan
- Backlog and sprint plan
- Acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Risk register and dependency log
- Retrospective action items

### Core Roles

- Project Manager (PM): coordinates schedule, risk, communication, and delivery health
- Product Manager / Product Lead: defines outcomes, prioritization, and success criteria
- Developers: implement features and maintain quality and testability
- QA / Testing: validate quality and acceptance criteria
- Stakeholders: provide direction, input, and approvals
