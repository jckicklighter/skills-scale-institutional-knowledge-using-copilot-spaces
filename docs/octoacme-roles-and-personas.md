# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- **Technical Lead / Architect**: align on design decisions and implementation approach
- **Product Manager**: clarify acceptance criteria and scope
- **Project Manager**: report progress, blockers, and dependencies
- **QA / Testing**: collaborate on testability and acceptance criteria validation
- **Release / DevOps Engineer**: coordinate on deployment and environment readiness

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: align on timeline, scope, and priority trade-offs
- **Technical Lead / Architect**: discuss feasibility and technical approach
- **UX / Product Designer**: collaborate on user needs, workflows, and usability
- **Business Analyst**: translate requirements into clear acceptance criteria
- **Executive Sponsor / Business Owner**: report on outcomes and strategic alignment
- **Customer Support / Operations**: incorporate operational readiness and customer feedback

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: align on priorities, scope, and timeline
- **Engineering Manager / Delivery Lead**: track capacity, dependencies, and delivery risks
- **Developers**: monitor progress, blockers, and dependencies
- **Release / DevOps Engineer**: coordinate release planning and deployment windows
- **Security / Compliance Partner**: manage security review and approval timelines
- **Executive Sponsor / Business Owner**: escalate risks and obtain approvals

---

## Engineering Manager / Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers or Delivery Leads own engineering capacity, technical delivery coordination, and implementation risks. They partner with the Project Manager on schedule and dependencies, and with the Product Manager on scope and trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Own team capacity planning and resource allocation
- Coordinate technical delivery across developers and teams
- Identify and escalate implementation and technical risks
- Remove blockers and facilitate cross-team collaboration
- Participate in scoping and feasibility assessments
- Support quality standards and process adherence

### Goals
- Enable team to deliver on commitments
- Maintain team velocity and sustainable pace
- Reduce technical debt and quality issues
- Foster collaborative and psychologically safe team environment

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and progress check-ins
- Weekly capacity and risk reviews with Project Manager
- Technical design discussions and architecture reviews
- One-on-ones with developers

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: coordinate on schedule, capacity, and risk escalation
- **Product Manager**: discuss scope, feasibility, and trade-offs
- **Developers**: lead technical direction, remove blockers, support growth
- **Technical Lead / Architect**: align on technical strategy and design decisions
- **QA / Testing**: coordinate on quality standards and testing approach

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical direction, design decisions, and technical quality. They work with Developers on implementation and with the Product Manager, Project Manager, and QA/Testing on feasibility, sequencing, and acceptance risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review designs and code for quality and maintainability
- Advise on technology choices and trade-offs
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers and support technical growth
- Ensure compliance with technical standards and best practices

### Goals
- Maintain scalable, maintainable, and secure architecture
- Reduce technical complexity and debt
- Enable team to build with confidence
- Support long-term product sustainability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and design consultations
- Risk and feasibility assessments
- Knowledge sharing and team mentoring

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: guide design, conduct code reviews, support implementation
- **Product Manager**: advise on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- **Project Manager**: communicate technical risks and sequencing impacts
- **Engineering Manager / Delivery Lead**: align on technical strategy
- **Security / Compliance Partner**: incorporate security and compliance requirements
- **QA / Testing**: advise on testability and acceptance criteria

---

## UX / Product Designer or Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/Product Designers or Researchers represent user needs through research, workflows, and usability validation. They collaborate with the Product Manager on outcomes, Developers on feasibility, and QA/Testing on usability-related acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather requirements
- Design workflows, interfaces, and user experiences
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate usability through testing and feedback
- Advocate for user needs and usability in all decisions
- Support QA/Testing on usability acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Maximize user satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce usability issues and support burden
- Ensure designs are feasible and implementable
- Build products that delight users

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and design rationale
- Design specifications and mockups
- Usability testing sessions and findings
- Design review and feedback sessions

### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: align on user needs, outcomes, and prioritization
- **Developers**: discuss design feasibility and implementation approach
- **Technical Lead / Architect**: review design feasibility and scalability
- **QA / Testing**: define usability acceptance criteria and test approaches
- **Engineering Manager / Delivery Lead**: coordinate on design and development schedule

---

## Business Analyst / Requirements Lead

### Role Summary
Business Analysts or Requirements Leads translate stakeholder needs into requirements, acceptance criteria, and traceable decisions. They support the Product Manager and Project Manager and coordinate clarification with Developers and QA/Testing.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and analyze stakeholder requirements
- Define clear, testable acceptance criteria
- Create requirements documentation and traceability matrices
- Facilitate clarification discussions between stakeholders and delivery teams
- Identify ambiguities and risks in requirements
- Track requirement changes and document rationale

### Goals
- Ensure clarity and traceability from business need to delivery
- Reduce rework and misalignment
- Minimize requirement ambiguities and assumptions
- Support quality and acceptance validation

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specifications
- Clarification sessions with stakeholders and teams
- Acceptance criteria reviews and feedback
- Traceability reports and change logs

### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: validate requirements align with product vision
- **Project Manager**: track requirement changes and impact to schedule
- **Developers**: clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- **QA / Testing**: define testable acceptance criteria and test strategies
- **Stakeholders**: gather requirements and validate alignment

---

## Release / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Release or DevOps Engineers own deployment automation, environment readiness, observability, and rollback execution. They coordinate with Developers, QA/Testing, the Project Manager, and stakeholders during release planning and verification.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment automation and CI/CD pipelines
- Manage production and staging environments
- Plan releases and coordinate deployment schedules
- Execute deployments and monitor rollout
- Implement observability, monitoring, and alerting
- Develop and execute rollback procedures
- Support incident response and post-deployment verification

### Goals
- Enable safe, reliable, and frequent releases
- Minimize deployment risk and downtime
- Maintain observability and incident response capability
- Reduce manual effort and human error in deployment

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Deployment runbooks and procedures
- Monitoring and alerting configurations
- Post-deployment verification and retrospectives

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: coordinate on deployment requirements and observability needs
- **QA / Testing**: coordinate on smoke testing and release verification
- **Project Manager**: align on release schedule and deployment windows
- **Security / Compliance Partner**: incorporate security scanning and compliance checks
- **Engineering Manager / Delivery Lead**: plan capacity and resource needs
- **Stakeholders**: communicate release status and incident impact

---

## Security or Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
Security or Compliance Partners identify security, privacy, and regulatory requirements and validate controls. They engage early with Product, Engineering, QA/Testing, and the Project Manager to manage security risks and approvals.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and compliance requirements early
- Review designs and code for security and privacy risks
- Conduct security scanning and penetration testing
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and policy standards
- Advise on secure architecture and best practices
- Manage security incident response and communication
- Provide security training and awareness

### Goals
- Build security and privacy into all deliverables
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Enable fast, secure releases
- Maintain stakeholder trust

### Typical Communication
- Security and compliance requirements and guidance
- Security review findings and recommendations
- Compliance assessment reports
- Security incident communication

### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: identify security and privacy outcomes
- **Technical Lead / Architect**: review architecture for security
- **Developers**: review code and support secure implementation
- **Project Manager**: track security review timelines and risks
- **QA / Testing**: validate security controls and acceptance criteria
- **Release / DevOps Engineer**: incorporate security scanning in CI/CD

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support or Operations Representatives bring operational readiness, support impact, and customer feedback into planning and release decisions. They work with Product, Project Management, QA/Testing, and Release/DevOps on communications and post-release follow-up.

### Responsibilities
- Represent customer and operational perspective in planning
- Identify operational readiness and support readiness needs
- Provide customer feedback and usage patterns to product planning
- Participate in release preparation and runbooks
- Support customer communication and training for new features
- Monitor customer impact and gather post-release feedback
- Identify support burden and suggest improvements

### Goals
- Minimize customer impact and support burden
- Ensure operational readiness and support capability
- Maximize customer satisfaction and adoption
- Identify improvement opportunities from customer feedback

### Typical Communication
- Operational readiness and support requirements
- Customer feedback and usage insights
- Release notes and customer communications
- Post-release customer impact and feedback

### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: provide customer feedback and usage insights
- **Project Manager**: identify operational and support readiness risks
- **QA / Testing**: validate customer-facing acceptance criteria
- **Release / DevOps Engineer**: coordinate customer communication and support readiness
- **Developers**: report customer issues and improvement ideas
- **Stakeholders**: communicate customer impact and satisfaction

---

## Executive Sponsor / Business Owner

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors or Business Owners provide strategic direction, funding, priority decisions, and escalation support. They partner with the Project Manager and Product Manager at decision gates and when risks exceed team-level authority.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic direction and business context
- Approve project charter and major scope changes
- Allocate funding and resources
- Make priority trade-off decisions
- Escalate and resolve business-level blockers
- Communicate progress to broader leadership
- Approve release to production for major initiatives

### Goals
- Align projects with business strategy
- Maximize business value and ROI
- Reduce business risk and uncertainty
- Enable fast decision-making and unblocking

### Typical Communication
- Project charter and approval
- Milestone and gate reviews
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Decision and escalation meetings

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: receive status, approve scope changes, resolve escalations
- **Product Manager**: align on outcomes and business priorities
- **Stakeholders**: communicate strategic progress and impact
- **Executive Leadership**: report on business results and strategic alignment

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Responsibility and interaction matrices can be created to show decision authority and handoffs across project lifecycle phases.
- In smaller projects or teams, one person may hold multiple roles; use these definitions to clarify which responsibilities and communication patterns apply.
